# CMA Part 2: supplied Study Units 1–7

Seven Gleim 2024 teaching outlines were integrated into the Part 2 curriculum. Their 26 sections have Arabic and English instructional guides, three titled lessons per section, worked calculations, formulas, common errors, six original bilingual practice questions per section and one linked case per unit. The third lesson expands subjects such as horizontal analysis, bond pricing, CAPM, financing costs, derivatives, factoring and inventory policy. Part 1 and Part 2 use separate unit identifiers, so the same unit number cannot mix questions in the library or exam tool.
