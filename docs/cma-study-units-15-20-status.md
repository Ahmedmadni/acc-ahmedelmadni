The site's complete Part 1 inventory is now 80 guided sections, 20 linked cases and 2,967 questions: 524 original bilingual questions plus the existing 2,443 English source-bank records from the two supplied Gleim 2025 volumes. Those source-bank questions preserve their source pages and status flags. Their Arabic translation and visual table review remain separately in progress. The 2024 outlines are teaching material, and the 92 new items are original applications rather than a transcription of copyrighted textbook questions.

Run `npm run cma:check-content` to check the section, question and case mapping, source-bank totals, language completeness and independently calculated answers. The source page range displayed with a guide refers to its 2024 outline; the source page range attached to a 2025 question refers to its question-bank volume.
